Common Questions

Kelowna Property Services — answered.

What areas do you cover?

Kelowna city core plus Mission, Glenmore and Lower Mission — most properties within reasonable driving distance. Call if you're unsure whether your address is in-zone.

How do recurring service plans work?

Same crew on a fixed schedule (quarterly or semi-annually depending on the service), locked-in pricing for the contract period, and priority response between visits. Pause or cancel with reasonable notice — no long-term lock-in.

Are you insured?

Yes — full liability insurance and WCB coverage on every job. Certificates available on request, which property managers and strata councils often need up front.

How is pricing structured?

Written quotes for every job after an on-site or virtual walkthrough. No surprise charges — quotes are valid for 30 days.

Do you work with stratas and property managers?

All the time. We provide certificates of insurance, schedule around quiet hours, and coordinate with on-site building managers.
